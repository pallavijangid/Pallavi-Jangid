DISRUPT THEPATTERN

This project started as a simple question: what happens when a landing page behaves less like a webpage and more like an experience? Animation became the main character here, supported by dynamic mobile interactions, oversized CTAs, and AI-generated 3D illustrations that constantly pushed the visuals into unfamiliar territory. The goal wasn’t just to make something flashy, but to understand how people respond to movement and interaction. It became a shared playground for both the design and development teams to experiment, fail fast, and figure out what actually makes users stay curious.
