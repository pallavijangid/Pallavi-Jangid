THE OFFICE : POSTER SERIES

A visual poster exploration inspired by everyday office culture, where routine objects and recurring moments became playful narrative anchors. The series focused on humor, rhythm, and typographic contrast.

I experimented with bold framing, color tension, and expressive compositing to create posters that felt familiar yet surprising, turning ordinary workspace cues into conversation-starting visuals.
