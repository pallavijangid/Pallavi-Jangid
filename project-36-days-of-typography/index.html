PALLAVI JANGID

36 DAYS OFTYPOGRAPHY

Probably the most personal project I've worked on, and definitely the one that taught me the most patience. Every letter started as a rough sketch, usually followed by adding little details inspired by my furry pet somewhere into the composition. From there, the illustrations were refined, broken into frames, and animated in After Effects to give each letter its own tiny personality.

Somewhere between day one and day thirty-six, the project quietly became a lesson in consistency, routine, and showing up even when inspiration didn't. Looking back, this is also where my obsession with typography probably began. Still sad the challenge got discontinued.
