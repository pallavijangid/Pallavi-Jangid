VISUAL IDENTITY

Founded by the Art Directors Club in 1996 as a showcase of emerging creative talent, ADC Young Guns is a portfolio-based competition for creative professionals 30 years of age or under that has grown to become one of the most coveted accolades in the global creative community.

Each year, The One Club for Creativity invites a past winner to define the visual identity for the next Young Guns class. As a YG19 alum, I was honored to lead the branding and award design.
