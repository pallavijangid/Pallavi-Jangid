Built the visual identity for the organization deck; the kind that quietly shows up everywhere. The style leaned heavily into industrial textures, bold layouts, and structured systems that could work across both internal and external presentations. I also explored AI-generated deck covers to give each presentation its own personality without losing consistency.

Right now, the project is evolving into something even more exciting: experimenting with a Claude skill that can automate slide creation while still keeping the brand language intact. Turns out even presentations deserve a design system and a little personality.
